Install in 4 steps

The app is distributed as an APK file, so Android needs permission to install it from outside the Play Store.

  1. Download the APK

    Tap the Download APK button above on your phone (or download on a PC and copy the file over via USB / cable).

  2. Allow installs from your browser

    Open Settings → Apps → Special access → Install unknown apps, choose your browser or file manager, and enable Allow from this source.

  3. Open the file and tap Install

    Open the downloaded health-quest-v4.39-debug.apk from your notifications or Downloads folder. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, choose Install anyway — it's a personal debug build.

  4. Finish onboarding

    Launch Health Quest, answer three quick setup questions (your goals, activity level and main struggle) and allow notifications, Usage Access and Health Connect when asked so reminders, screen-time and step sync work.

From a PC with USB debugging: run adb install health-quest-v4.39-debug.apk. Updating from an older version keeps your data; installing over it just works.

App tour

Every screen answers one question, and everything saves as you go.

🏠

Today

How am I doing, and what's next? Your score and grade, the four category bars, one suggested next action you can tap, a glass that fills as you log water, a sleep timer button, and four buttons to log a meal, steps, sleep or weight. Unwell? One tap sets the day aside and your streak pauses.

🍽️

Log · Food

Each meal lists what you ate with its time. Tap Add on a meal to pick foods, repeat yesterday's meal or reuse a favourite. Fasting, calories and drinks sit underneath. Water, and any drink you log in a meal such as coffee or juice, count toward an estimated daily goal based on your weight, and never affect the score.

👟

Log · Move

Steps (synced from Health Connect, or typed in), plus switches for a post-dinner walk and an exercise session, with an estimate of the calories it burned.

🌙

Log · Sleep

Start the timer at bedtime and stop it when you wake. Screen time is deducted automatically. Times, quality and notes are under Edit details.

📅

Progress

Week gives you one thing to fix. Month is a colour-coded calendar — tap a day for full detail or share it as an image. Trends has the charts, meal insights and all-time stats.

⚖️

Weight

Log daily weight and watch the trend line approach your target, whether you are losing, keeping or gaining: 7-day average, change vs last week, and 30d / 90d / 1y views.

🧑‍🚀

Profile

Your level and XP, the next badge you are closest to, today's and this week's goals, and every badge you have earned.

🧩

Home-screen widgets

Eight small widgets in the app's own light or dark look. Four to see: today's score and next step, the week, calories and goals. Four to log without opening the app: a glass of water, the sleep timer, quick links to log a meal, move, sleep or weight, and today's weight. Add them from Settings → Widgets.

Settings

The gear on Profile. Four groups: you, scoring rules, food & AI, and reminders & data (Excel export and JSON backup).

Gamification & coaching

Progress is meant to feel rewarding, not like a spreadsheet.

🏅

100+ badges

Seven categories — Consistency, Nutrition, Fitness, Sleep, Comeback, Hydration, Elite — plus hidden badges and three combination badges. Habit badges can be earned again and again.

⚡

Levels & XP

Every logged day earns XP from your score, and so do daily and weekly goals. Level up and keep a streak alive (quality days count).

🔔

Reminders that look first

Choose Quiet, Standard or Coach. Standard sends at most three a day (bedtime, dinner and one nudge); Coach about six. Nothing is sent once the job is done or on a sick day, and tapping one opens the right screen. Nudges draw on your own logs, and an optional AI key can word them for you, with no change in how many you get.

🎯

Your body goal

Lose, keep or gain weight, with or without building muscle. Your calorie target, protein range, scoring, badges and colours all follow it.

🔥

Net calories

Food eaten minus an estimate of everything you burned. See it by day, week and month, then check it against what the scale actually did, and correct your maintenance figure with one tap.

🤖

AI health summary

Optional. Add a free API key in Settings and generate a per-day diet / sleep / workout brief with tomorrow's priorities. Without a key, everything else still works.

FAQ

Where is my data stored?

Only on your phone, in the app's local database. Nothing is uploaded unless you turn on an AI feature, and even then only what that feature needs. Use Settings → Export & backup to save a JSON file and restore it on a new device.

Do the widgets work with the app closed?

Yes. A glass of water shows on the widget straight away, and the sleep timer runs on the home screen. Anything tapped on a widget is kept on your phone and added to your logs the next time you open the app, with the time you tapped it. Scores update then. Calories and Weight show personal numbers, so add them only if you are happy for anyone who sees your home screen to see them.

Do I need internet?

No. Only the optional AI features (health summary, food lookup and AI-written nudges) need a connection. AI-written nudges are off by default. When on, they send summary figures such as average scores and usual times to your AI provider about once a day, and never your name, notes, foods or body measurements. You can see exactly what is sent under Settings → Reminders.

Why does Android warn me during install?

The APK is a debug build shared outside the Play Store, so Play Protect doesn't recognise it. Only install it from a source you trust.

Steps aren't syncing — what do I check?

Make sure Health Connect is installed and that Health Quest has permission to read Steps. Sleep screen-time deduction needs Usage Access enabled for the app.

Reminders don't fire on time.

Allow notifications, and set battery usage for Health Quest to Unrestricted on phones with aggressive battery savers. A delay of a few minutes is normal, because Android groups alarms together to save battery.

Can I export my data?

Yes — Settings has a full Excel export (scores, diet, workouts, sleep, weight) and a JSON backup.
